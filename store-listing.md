## English (en-US)

**App name** (max 30)

Facturescu: bills & utilities

**Short description** (max 80)

All your Romanian bills in one place, with due-date reminders. No subscription.

**Full description** (max 4000)

Facturescu keeps track of your bills for you. Connect your online accounts with your providers, and the app fetches the amount and due date of every new bill and reminds you in time to pay.

WHAT IT DOES
• Fetches bills automatically from PPC, E.ON, Hidroelectrica, Digi, Orange, Netflix, Aquatim, Aqua Dumbrăvița, Platformis (building maintenance) and Ghișeul.ro (local taxes, ANAF).
• Reminds you before the due date and tells you when a new bill arrives.
• The Pay button opens the provider's own page, already signed in, so you can pay in seconds.
• Add any other bill by hand: rent, insurance, loans, subscriptions.
• Full payment history, month by month.
• Stats by category, with a chart for each month.
• Several homes? Filter bills by address.
• Light or dark theme, in Romanian or English.

YOUR DATA STAYS WITH YOU
• You sign in on each provider's own page. Your password goes only to the provider; Facturescu never sees it.
• Your bills and history stay on your phone. No servers, no ads, no tracking.
• You pay on the provider's site; card details never pass through the app.

FREE OR PRO
The free version includes unlimited bills, reminders, the Pay button, 2 connected provider accounts and the last 3 months of history and stats.
Facturescu Pro adds unlimited connected accounts, filtering by address and your full history. One-time purchase, no subscription.

Facturescu is not affiliated with or endorsed by the providers shown. Their names and logos belong to their owners.

## Answers for the Play Console forms

**Data safety**
- Data collected: none. Bills, payments, addresses and settings are stored only on the device and never sent to the developer.
- Data shared: none.
- Data encrypted in transit: yes (every connection is HTTPS).
- Users can delete their data: yes (delete in the app, or uninstall / clear app data).
- Note for the reviewer, if asked: the app reads bills from provider websites the user signs in to, on the device; nothing goes to servers of ours.

**Financial features declaration**
The app shows the user's utility bills and opens the provider's own website to pay. It does not process payments, hold funds, offer loans or give financial advice.

**App access** (instructions for reviewers)
No account is needed to use the app. Tap „Adaugă factură” to add a bill by hand; reminders, history, stats and the Pro screen all work with bills added this way. Connecting a provider account needs the user's own account with that Romanian provider, so it can't be shown with demo credentials.

**Ads**: the app contains no ads.

**Target audience**: 18 and over.

**Content rating**: utility app, no user-generated content, no sharing of location or personal data.
